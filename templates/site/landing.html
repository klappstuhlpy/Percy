Economy & items

A server economy members actually use: items with effects, a shop and an inventory.

  • Items with effects, rarity and optional stock limits or role requirements.
  • A shop and an inventory members browse in Discord, with trading between members.
  • Balances, daily rewards and games, all adjustable from the dashboard.

Leveling & rank cards

XP from chatting, rewards for climbing, and a rank card members can make their own.

  • Reward roles, XP multipliers and blacklists per server.
  • Rank cards with theme, accent, bar and font presets; admins can set a default or lock it.
  • Level-up messages with your own template and a leaderboard on the dashboard.

Moderation & notifications

Keep a record of every action, and let your helpers ask instead of guessing.

  • Kick, ban, timeout, warn and purge, each recorded as a case with a reason.
  • Helpers file action requests that a moderator approves or denies with one button.

Music

A player panel in your channel, with controls that work from Discord and the dashboard.

  • Queue, loop, shuffle, seek and volume from the panel buttons.
  • Equalizer and filters, plus an always-on mode for a voice channel.
  • Time-synced lyrics for the track that is playing.

AI assistant optional

Percy can answer questions about itself using a self-hosted model. It is off by default.

  • Each AI feature is a per-server switch, with per-channel overrides.
  • Ask how to set something up and get an answer based on Percy's real command list.
  • When it is off or unavailable, every command behaves exactly as it always did.
