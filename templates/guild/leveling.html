Members earn XP for messages (and, optionally, for time spent active in voice) and level up over time. Tune the XP curve, level-up announcements, reward roles, multipliers and blacklists below — everything is applied instantly and cached by the bot.

  • Voice XP skips members who are alone, AFK or deafened
  • Reward roles can stack or replace as levels rise
  • Blacklist roles, channels or users to exclude them from XP
  • Members check their card in Discord with /level
